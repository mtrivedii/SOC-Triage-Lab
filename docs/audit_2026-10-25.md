## 5. Draft "Free-tier limitations" paragraph (README)
> This project uses only free-tier LLM endpoints, which bounds the size of the evaluation. Google AI
> Studio's free tier for `gemini-3.8-flash` allows 20 generate-content requests per day (observed
> 2026-10-22); Groq's documented free limits for `openai/gpt-oss-120b` are 30 req/min, 1,000 req/day,
> 8,000 tokens/min and 200,000 tokens/day (not yet observed live). The pipeline sends exactly one LLM
> call per deterministic alert — 5 alerts across the 9 labelled cases; the 4 benign cases raise no
> alert and are never sent to a model. The evaluation is therefore deliberately demo-sized: 3 runs per
> alert on Groq (15 calls) and 2 per alert on Gemini, reporting the run-to-run spread rather than a
> single best run. Every call logs token counts and an "as-if-paid" cost estimate (currently $0 on the
> free tier). All reported numbers come from `results/runs.jsonl`; a wrong-but-plausible ATT&CK
> technique (e.g. the model tagging a web directory scan as T1046 rather than T1595.003) is counted as
> a technique error even though the verdict and attack type are correct.
